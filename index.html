Bathroom Party Video Maker

Import a Spotify playlist or upload audio files and a picture. Everything gets joined into one track that sounds like it's playing at a party while you're in the bathroom — muffled music through the wall, people talking, doors opening and closing — and comes back as an MP4 video (or just the MP3) you can watch and download. It all runs in your browser; nothing gets uploaded anywhere.

    Get the full songs — helper script runs on your computer

    Browsers aren't allowed to download whole songs from Spotify or YouTube — but this tiny Python script can. It reads the playlist and saves each song's audio from YouTube (the classic “metadata + YouTube” method, like spotify-dl, but with no account and no API keys). Files are named 01 - Artist - Title, so they snap into the playlist order automatically when you drop them in.

    1. One-time setup: install yt-dlp, and download spotify_helper.py (it sits next to this page — keep it anywhere on your computer).
    2. Run it with your playlist link: python3 spotify_helper.py "https://open.spotify.com/playlist/…"
    3. Drop the spotify-tracks folder it creates into step 2 — done.

    The script also saves playlist.json. If the import above ever fails, to get the track list offline instead. For personal use — please respect Spotify's & YouTube's terms and support the artists.
